🕯️ Lamplight Reading

How this works

You are the tutor; the app is your coach. Each night: 2 min warm-up, 6–8 min reading together, 2–3 min talking about it, then a quick note in the log. About 10–15 minutes.

  • No coins, streaks, ads, or autoplay. Skipping a night is fine.
  • No accounts. Everything is saved only in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Read-aloud check uses your browser's speech recognition where available (Chrome/Edge). In Chrome the audio may be processed by the browser vendor's servers. It's optional, and you decide what counts as a miss. It does not work in Brave (Brave blocks the speech service) or Firefox, so use Chrome or Edge, or just tap words by hand.
  • Clear and catchy: each story has one short, rhythmic "say it together" line and a picture-like comparison. Short beats and vivid images are easier to remember than long explanations, and the goal is for her to feel smart, not for the story to sound smart. Read it together twice at the end.
  • ⭐ Solo lets a child read alone: tap a word to hear it, "Read to me" highlights word by word, then a few tap-to-answer questions earn stars. 🔤 Letters practices matching big and little letters. Both save on this device only. Read-aloud uses your browser's built-in voice.
  • Tap any word while she reads to mark it as tricky. Tap it again to clear it.
